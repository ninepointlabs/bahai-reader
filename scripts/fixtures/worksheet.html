Study Workbook Sample

Section 1: Reading together

This sample page was written for testing a reader app. It has ordinary paragraphs, questions with blanks to fill in, ruled lines for longer answers, an open space with no lines, and a small table whose borders are not answer lines.

A study group meets each week to read a short passage, talk about what it means, and write their answers in the spaces below each question.

1. The group meets every ________ to read and ________ together.

2. In your own words, what does the group do after reading the passage?

3. Write down one thought you would like to share with the group.
